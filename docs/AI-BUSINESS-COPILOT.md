## English — What is Business Operating Copilot?

### Definition

**Business Operating Copilot** is an AI assistant that acts as:

1. **Planner** — Help set goals, break into steps, estimate timeline
2. **Advisor** — Recommend actions based on business data
3. **Navigator** — Guide execution of business journeys
4. **Executor** — Automate routine tasks (categorization, alerts, recommendations)
5. **Monitor** — Track KPIs, predict risks, alert anomalies
6. **Optimizer** — Suggest improvements based on performance

### NOT a Chatbot

**Chatbots:** Answer questions. User asks → Bot responds.

**Business Copilot:** Proactively helps users achieve business goals. Anticipates needs, recommends actions, monitors progress.

| Aspect | Chatbot | Business Copilot |
|---|---|---|
| **Initiation** | User-driven ("Ask me anything") | AI-driven (proactive recommendations) |
| **Output** | Conversational answers | Actionable recommendations + alerts |
| **Learning** | Stateless (each chat independent) | Stateful (learns from user's business) |
| **Value** | Information retrieval | Business outcome improvement |
| **Interaction** | Chat | Chat + UI recommendations + notifications |

### Six Roles

#### 1. Planner
**When:** User has a business goal ("I want to enter US market")  
**What it does:**
- Break goal into steps
- Estimate timeline
- Forecast costs
- Identify prerequisites
- Link to playbooks

**Example:**
```
User: "I want to sell headphones on Amazon US"
Copilot: "8-step journey, 30 days, ~$5,000 investment
         - Research market
         - Find suppliers
         - Register business
         - Open bank account
         - Set up Amazon seller
         - Inventory
         - First marketing
         - Monitor & optimize"
```

#### 2. Advisor
**When:** User faces a business decision  
**What it does:**
- Analyze available data
- Present options
- Recommend based on historical performance
- Alert to risks

**Example:**
```
Inventory: "Hôm nay tôi nên làm gì để tăng lợi nhuận?" (What should I do today to increase profit?)
Copilot shows 5 recommendations:
  1. Reduce price on TikTok shop (high volume, low margin) → shift to Shopee (high margin)
  2. Add 12 new products in trending category
  3. Reach out to 12 VIP customers for reorder
  4. Request supplier discount (MOQ >500)
  5. Optimize shipping (consolidate orders, reduce cost)
```

#### 3. Navigator
**When:** User is executing a business journey  
**What it does:**
- Guide to next step
- Provide resources/templates
- Flag blockers
- Suggest workarounds
- Celebrate milestones

**Example:**
```
Business Journey: Entering US market (Step 3: Find Suppliers)
Copilot:
  "You're on Step 3 of 8. Here are the top 5 suppliers for your product:
   - Supplier A: Rating 4.7/5, MOQ 100, $2.10-$2.80/unit
   - Supplier B: Rating 4.6/5, MOQ 500, $1.95-$2.50/unit
   [Would you like to request samples?]"
```

#### 4. Executor
**When:** Routine business tasks need doing  
**What it does:**
- Auto-categorize transactions
- Flag inventory warnings
- Schedule follow-ups
- Generate reports
- Send notifications

**Example:**
- "Đơn hàng từ 23 khách hàng mới arrived. Tôi đã gửi welcome email + upsell offer."
- "Tồn kho TikTok shop sắp hết (5 đơn vị). Tôi đã yêu cầu supplier gấp hàng."
- "Monthly report ready: +18.6% revenue vs last month."

#### 5. Monitor
**When:** Business is running; need to spot issues  
**What it does:**
- Track key metrics
- Predict cash flow changes
- Alert to anomalies
- Forecast impacts

**Example:**
```
ALERT: "Revenue TikTok Shop down 30% week-on-week. Possible causes:
  - Competitor discount detected
  - Seasonal slowdown expected (weekend)
  - Product review drop (4.8 → 4.5 stars)
  
  Recommendations:
  1. Launch flash sale (recover volume)
  2. Respond to negative reviews
  3. Reach out to top customer for feedback"
```

#### 6. Optimizer
**When:** Business is stable; looking for improvements  
**What it does:**
- Analyze performance data
- Benchmark vs similar sellers
- Identify quick wins
- Suggest scaling strategies

**Example:**
```
ANALYSIS: "Your Amazon sales are strong (4.8/5 rating, 1,256 units/month).
  You're outperforming 87% of sellers in this category.
  
  Next opportunities:
  1. Expand to Amazon UK (same product, 35% cheaper sourcing)
  2. Add complementary product (phone holders) → cross-sell
  3. Private label (white-label supplier) → 3x margin vs resale"
```

### Copilot Interface

**Where Users See Copilot:**

1. **Home Dashboard** — Greeting + summary of today's opportunities
2. **Task Chat** — "Ask me anything about your business"
3. **Recommendations** — "Tổng Tài recommends 5 actions today"
4. **Notifications** — Alerts, achievements, milestones
5. **In-Context** — Each screen has copilot suggestions
6. **Journey Guide** — Step-by-step guidance within journeys

**What Copilot Shows:**
- Short, actionable recommendations (not essays)
- Data sources (why this recommendation)
- Impact prediction (if you do this, revenue +X%)
- Playbook links (see how others did this)
- User controls (accept/dismiss/customize)

### AI Behaviors

| Scenario | Copilot Behavior |
|---|---|
| User opens app after 3 days | "Welcome back! Revenue up 12% while you were away. 3 new opportunities found." |
| Revenue drops 20% | Alert: "Revenue anomaly detected. [Diagnose]" |
| Inventory running low | "Kho HCM sắp hết (5 units). Yêu cầu supplier hàng?" |
| High-margin opportunity found | "Found high-ROI opportunity: Quạt mini trending +210%, Profit $8.45/unit" |
| Customer churn detected | "3 VIP customers haven't ordered in 30 days. Offer them 15% discount?" |
| Playbook available | "12 sellers entered Japan market. View successful playbook?" |

### Business Rules

1. **Proactive Not Reactive** — Copilot suggests before user asks
2. **Data-Driven** — All recommendations backed by data
3. **Respectful of User Time** — Max 5 recommendations/day
4. **Reversible Actions** — User always in control (review before execute)
5. **Learning Loop** — Copilot learns from user's choices
6. **Transparency** — Show confidence score + data sources
7. **No Dark Patterns** — Never manipulate user into unprofitable action

### Privacy & Data

- Copilot analysis happens on-device where possible
- Cloud analysis only for:
  - Market trend data (public sources)
  - Supplier research (public platforms)
  - Cross-user benchmarking (anonymized)
- User data never sold or monetized
- User can always opt out of specific recommendations

## Tiếng Việt — AI Tổng Tài Là Gì?

### Định Nghĩa

**AI Tổng Tài** là trợ lý AI đóng vai trò:

1. **Lập Kế Hoạch** — Giúp đặt mục tiêu, chia thành bước, ước tính thời gian
2. **Tư Vấn** — Đề xuất hành động dựa trên dữ liệu kinh doanh
3. **Dẫn Đường** — Hướng dẫn thực thi hành trình kinh doanh
4. **Thực Thi** — Tự động hóa tác vụ thường xuyên
5. **Giám Sát** — Theo dõi KPI, dự báo rủi ro, cảnh báo bất thường
6. **Tối Ưu Hóa** — Đề xuất cải thiện dựa trên hiệu suất

### KHÔNG Phải Chatbot

Xem bảng so sánh tiếng Anh ở trên.

### Sáu Vai Trò

Xem chi tiết tiếng Anh ở trên cho mỗi vai trò với ví dụ.

### Quy Tắc Kinh Doanh

1. **Chủ Động Không Phản Ứng** — Copilot gợi ý trước khi người dùng hỏi
2. **Dựa Trên Dữ Liệu** — Tất cả đề xuất được hỗ trợ bởi dữ liệu
3. **Tôn Trọng Thời Gian Người Dùng** — Tối đa 5 đề xuất/ngày
4. **Hành Động Có Thể Đảo Ngược** — Người dùng luôn kiểm soát
5. **Vòng Lặp Học Tập** — Copilot học từ lựa chọn của người dùng
6. **Minh Bạch** — Hiển thị điểm tin cậy + nguồn dữ liệu
7. **Không Dark Pattern** — Không bao giờ thao túng người dùng

### Riêng Tư & Dữ Liệu

- Phân tích Copilot diễn ra trên thiết bị nơi có thể
- Phân tích cloud chỉ cho:
  - Dữ liệu xu hướng thị trường (nguồn công khai)
  - Nghiên cứu nhà cung cấp (nền tảng công khai)
  - Điểm chuẩn xuyên người dùng (ẩn danh)
- Dữ liệu người dùng không bao giờ bán hoặc kiếm tiền
- Người dùng có thể luôn từ chối các đề xuất cụ thể
