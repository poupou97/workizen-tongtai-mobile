# User Personas & Journey Maps

## Nhân Vật Người Dùng & Bản Đồ Hành Trình

---

## English — User Personas & Goals

**Purpose:** This document defines target user personas for Tổng Tài and maps their goals, frustrations, and success paths through the product.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary User Segment:** Entrepreneurs and SME business owners in Vietnam and Southeast Asia  
**Business Types:** E-commerce sellers, retailers, importers, FMCG distributors  
**Revenue Range:** $100K - $10M annual revenue  
**Team Size:** 1-50 people  
**Tech Proficiency:** Low to Medium  
**Decision Maker:** Business owner or operations manager

---

## Persona 1: Phương — E-Commerce Seller (Shopee Native)

### Profile

**Name:** Phương  
**Age:** 32  
**Business:** Shopee seller (stitched apparel)  
**Annual Revenue:** $500K  
**Team:** 2 (herself + 1 warehouse staff)  
**Tech Level:** Medium (knows Excel, Shopee seller tools)  

**Background:**  
Phương sells stitched apparel (áo thun, áo sơmi) on Shopee. Started 3 years ago with $5K. Has 2,000 repeat customers. Manages everything on her phone: inventory tracking in Google Sheets, photos in Google Drive, messages via Shopee app. Pain point: Switching between 5 different apps.

### Business Context

**Primary Revenue:** Shopee (85%), Facebook (10%), TikTok Shop (5%)  
**Inventory:** 50 SKUs (mostly apparel variants)  
**Customers:** 2,000 repeat, 500 new per month  
**Challenges:**
- Lost sales due to stockouts (no real-time stock sync across warehouse)
- Manual pricing across 3 channels (Shopee, Facebook, TikTok)
- Doesn't know which customers are most valuable
- Misses market trends (doesn't know what's trending to stock)

### User Goals (Why She Uses Tổng Tài)

#### Goal 1: "Expand to US Market"
**Motivation:** Shopee margins shrinking. Wants 20% of revenue from US Amazon.  
**Success Criteria:** First Amazon sale in 90 days, $5K/month revenue by Month 6.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Producer:** Find US suppliers for apparel, research market, arbitrage analysis
- **Journey:** "Enter US market" guided plan (8 steps)
- **Finance:** Track US revenue separately, profit margins
- **Copilot:** Answer questions: "How do I set up FBA?", "What are logistics costs?"

**Expected Outcome:**  
With Tổng Tài guidance, Phương identifies 3 quality US suppliers, determines her landed costs, sets up Amazon account + FBA in 60 days, launches 20 SKUs, and achieves $3K revenue by Month 4.

---

#### Goal 2: "Optimize Inventory to Reduce Stockouts"
**Motivation:** Lost 10 sales last month due to "out of stock". Wants to maintain 80% fill rate.  
**Success Criteria:** Stockout events < 2% of orders, inventory turnover > 6x/year.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Inventory:** Real-time stock across warehouse, reorder alerts
- **Reports:** Stock turnover analytics by SKU
- **Copilot:** "Which products are slow-moving?", "When should I reorder?"

**Expected Outcome:**  
With real-time inventory and AI alerts, Phương reduces stockouts by 80%, improves cash flow (faster turnover), and increases customer satisfaction (on-time delivery).

---

#### Goal 3: "Grow US Customers via Loyalty Program"
**Motivation:** US customers have low repeat rate (12% vs 60% in Vietnam). Wants $2K/month LTV.  
**Success Criteria:** Repeat purchase rate > 40%, avg LTV > $300.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Consumer:** Customer segmentation, segment campaigns
- **Reports:** LTV by segment, repeat purchase rate
- **Copilot:** Segment recommendations ("Target low-price buyers with premium tier offers")

**Expected Outcome:**  
By targeting repeat customers with loyalty offers, Phương grows US repeat purchase rate from 12% to 35%, LTV increases from $50 to $200, and revenue becomes more predictable.

---

## Persona 2: Tuấn — Inventory Manager (Distribution Hub)

### Profile

**Name:** Tuấn  
**Age:** 38  
**Business:** Apparel distributor (for retailers)  
**Annual Revenue:** $3M  
**Team:** 8 (1 sales, 3 warehouse, 2 admin, 1 accountant, 1 manager)  
**Tech Level:** Low (uses email, WhatsApp, paper inventory sheets)  

**Background:**  
Tuấn runs a distribution hub for stitched apparel, selling to 120+ retail shops. Has 500 SKUs across 3 warehouses. Currently uses paper inventory sheets + Excel spreadsheets. Pain point: Inventory mismatches between sheets and actual stock cause constant headaches.

### Business Context

**Primary Revenue:** Retail shop sales (wholesale)  
**Inventory:** 500+ SKUs, 3 warehouses (Hanoi, Danang, HCMC)  
**Customers:** 120 retail shops  
**Suppliers:** 5 apparel manufacturers  
**Challenges:**
- Inventory discrepancies between 3 warehouses (paper tracking breaks down)
- Slow order processing (takes 3 days to confirm stock + deliver)
- No visibility into which products are dying/best-sellers across regions
- Can't forecast demand when retailers make sudden large orders

### User Goals

#### Goal 1: "Eliminate Inventory Mismatches"
**Motivation:** Lost $50K revenue last year due to false "no stock" responses + delayed deliveries.  
**Success Criteria:** Inventory match rate > 98%, no reconciliation errors.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Inventory:** Real-time stock across 3 warehouses
- **Reports:** Inventory accuracy dashboard
- **Copilot:** "Why is Hanoi stock different from system?" (auto-audit)

**Expected Outcome:**  
With real-time Tổng Tài inventory, Tuấn can confidently confirm orders in <1 hour (vs 3 days), reduce lost sales by $40K/year, and eliminate warehouse staff's manual reconciliation work.

---

#### Goal 2: "Optimize Warehouse Space & Stocking"
**Motivation:** Paying $8K/month for warehouse space. Some products use 40% of space but generate only 5% of revenue.  
**Success Criteria:** Space utilization > 85%, ROI per m² > 80K/year.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Inventory:** Stock levels, turnover rates by product
- **Reports:** Revenue per SKU, space efficiency analysis
- **Copilot:** "Which SKUs should I discontinue?" (dead stock analysis)

**Expected Outcome:**  
Tổng Tài analysis shows Tuấn that 40 SKUs are slow movers (< 2 turns/year). He discontinues them, frees up 600 m² of space, reduces warehouse cost to $5K/month, saves $36K/year.

---

#### Goal 3: "Forecast Demand by Region"
**Motivation:** Retailers often place large, unpredictable orders. Tuấn wants to avoid stockouts.  
**Success Criteria:** Demand forecast accuracy MAPE < 20%, stockout frequency < 2%.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Inventory:** Historical sales by region
- **Reports:** Trend forecasting (AI predicts next month's demand)
- **Copilot:** "Hanoi retailers usually buy more in Q4 — prepare stock now"

**Expected Outcome:**  
With Tổng Tài forecasting, Tuấn proactively stocks warehouses, reduces stockouts from 8% to <1%, improves retailer satisfaction (on-time fills), and increases repeat orders.

---

## Persona 3: Hoa — Finance Manager (Family Business)

### Profile

**Name:** Hoa  
**Age:** 42  
**Business:** Retail network (6 shops, family business)  
**Annual Revenue:** $2.5M  
**Team:** 30 total (shop staff + operations)  
**Tech Level:** Low (Excel user)  

**Background:**  
Hoa manages finances for her family's retail business: 6 clothing shops across Vietnam + small online presence. Daily challenge: doesn't know true profitability or cash flow status. Spends 5+ hours/week on manual financial reconciliation.

### Business Context

**Revenue Streams:** 6 shop locations (60%), online/Shopee (25%), wholesale to other retailers (15%)  
**Expenses:** Staff salaries (35%), rent (25%), COGS (28%), utilities (5%), marketing (7%)  
**Cash Flow Issue:** Spends $80K on restocking before knowing if previous stock sold  
**Challenges:**
- No unified view of 6 shops' finances
- Manual daily reconciliation takes forever
- Doesn't know which shop is most/least profitable
- Can't forecast cash needs or see profitability by product

### User Goals

#### Goal 1: "Get Daily Cash Flow Visibility"
**Motivation:** Currently checks shop reconciliation sheets daily (1 hour/day). Needs 10-minute dashboard view.  
**Success Criteria:** Daily cash flow view in <1 min, forecast accuracy > 90%.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Finance:** Unified dashboard (revenue, expenses, profit, cash)
- **Reports:** Cash flow forecast (7-day, 30-day)
- **Copilot:** "When should I expect cash from credit sales?"

**Expected Outcome:**  
Hoa logs in each morning, sees cash balance, forecast, and any risks (e.g., "Large expense next week"). Makes restocking decisions confidently instead of guessing.

---

#### Goal 2: "Identify Profitable vs Loss-Making Shops"
**Motivation:** Suspects Shop 3 (Da Nang) is losing money, but doesn't have data.  
**Success Criteria:** Profitability by shop visible, identify worst-performing shop.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Finance:** Revenue/expenses broken down by shop
- **Reports:** Profitability analysis by location
- **Copilot:** "Shop 3 loses $1K/month. Should you close it?"

**Expected Outcome:**  
Tổng Tài confirms Shop 3 has 8% net margin (vs 12% average). Hoa renegotiates rent, adjusts staffing, improves to 11% margin. Or if unfixable, closes shop and redirects resources to profitable shops.

---

#### Goal 3: "Optimize Inventory Investment"
**Motivation:** Currently spends $250K/month on restocking (guess-based). Wants to reduce to $200K without losing sales.  
**Success Criteria:** Reduce inventory investment by 20%, maintain fill rate > 95%.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Inventory:** Stock levels, turnover rates per SKU
- **Finance:** Inventory cost analysis
- **Reports:** ROI per SKU, dead stock identification
- **Copilot:** "Which products sell slowest? Consider discontinuing."

**Expected Outcome:**  
Tổng Tài shows Hoa that $50K/month goes to slow-moving inventory. She discontinues unpopular SKUs, focuses on top 80 products, reduces inventory investment to $200K/month, and improves cash flow by $50K/month.

---

## Persona 4: Anh Minh — Growth-Focused Seller (Multi-Channel)

### Profile

**Name:** Anh Minh  
**Age:** 28  
**Business:** E-commerce & retail (Shopee, TikTok, Facebook, own web)  
**Annual Revenue:** $1.8M  
**Team:** 5 (1 sales, 2 warehouse, 1 customer service, 1 operations)  
**Tech Level:** High (uses multiple platforms, data-driven)  

**Background:**  
Anh Minh is an ambitious e-commerce entrepreneur. Sells on 4 channels simultaneously. Wants to scale to $10M in 3 years. Currently uses 7 different tools (Shopee, TikTok Shop, Metabase, Google Sheets, Mailchimp, Stripe, WooCommerce). Pain point: No unified business view.

**Revenue:** Shopee (40%), TikTok (30%), Facebook (20%), own website (10%)  
**Inventory:** 200 SKUs (many variants)  
**Customers:** 5K repeat, 2K new per month  
**Annual Growth:** 30% MoM (wants 50%)  
**Challenges:**
- Can't see unified profitability (each channel has different margins)

---

#### Goal 3: "Grow Customer LTV to 4x"

**Success Criteria:** LTV grow from $80 to $300 via repeat purchases + upsell.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Consumer:** LTV calculation by customer, segment analysis
- **Reports:** Repeat purchase rate, churn prediction
- **Copilot:** "Target repeat customers with loyalty program"

**Expected Outcome:**  
Tổng Tài identifies that VIP customers (LTV > $500) also have highest repeat rate (60%). Anh Minh targets VIP customers with 15% loyalty discount, grows repeat rate from 20% to 45%, increases LTV from $80 to $200 in 6 months.

## Persona 5: Lan — Operations Manager (Startup Mode)

### Profile

**Name:** Lan  
**Age:** 26  
**Business:** New startup (just launched 6 months ago, apparel subscription)  
**Annual Revenue:** $200K (projected $1M if scaling works)  
**Team:** 3 (founder, herself as ops manager, 1 part-time)  
**Tech Level:** High (startup mentality, data-driven)  

**Background:**  
Lan is operations manager for an early-stage apparel subscription startup. Bootstrapped with $20K. Trying to reach $10K/month revenue. Currently uses Google Sheets for everything. Pain point: Can't track what's working vs. what's wasting cash.

### Business Context

**Revenue Model:** $29/month subscription, 700 active subscribers ($20K/month)  
**Inventory:** 50 unique monthly designs, 500 variants  
**Customers:** 700 active subscribers, 200/month churn  
**Burn Rate:** $15K/month (needs profitable by Month 12)  
**Challenges:**
- Doesn't know which customer will churn (retention crisis)
- Doesn't track cost per customer acquisition
- Can't forecast cash runway
- Needs to show investors clean metrics, but only has spreadsheets

### User Goals

#### Goal 1: "Predict Churn & Reduce Customer Attrition"
**Motivation:** Losing 200 customers/month (28% churn). Needs <15% churn to be profitable.  
**Success Criteria:** Churn prediction accuracy > 75%, reduce churn to <15%.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Consumer:** Churn risk scoring per customer
- **Reports:** Churn trends, retention analysis
- **Copilot:** "These 50 customers at high churn risk. Send retention offer."

**Expected Outcome:**  
Tổng Tài identifies that customers who skip 2 months have 80% churn risk. Lan proactively sends retention offers, reduces churn to 18%, improves MRR stability.

---

#### Goal 2: "Track CAC & LTV for Unit Economics"
**Motivation:** Spending $5 on acquisition, but doesn't know if $29 LTV is sustainable.  
**Success Criteria:** CAC < $15, LTV > $100, LTV:CAC ratio > 3:1.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Finance:** Track acquisition spend, COGS, fulfillment costs
- **Consumer:** LTV calculation (lifetime revenue - churn projection)
- **Reports:** Unit economics dashboard

**Expected Outcome:**  
Tổng Tài analysis shows: CAC = $5, but LTV (after churn) = $85 (not $29). LTV:CAC = 1.7:1 (not profitable). Lan realizes she needs to reduce churn OR grow LTV. Focuses on retention, improves LTV to $150, achieves 3:1 ratio, reaches profitability.

---

#### Goal 3: "Plan for Fundraising (Clean Metrics)"
**Motivation:** Wants to raise $500K Series A. Investors want clear metrics, projections, unit economics.  
**Success Criteria:** Investor-ready financial model, validated growth metrics.  
**Tổng Tài Supports:**
- **Finance:** Clean financials (P&L, cash flow projections)
- **Reports:** Growth trajectory, retention curves, LTV trends
- **Copilot:** "Here are your key metrics for investors: MRR $20K, growth 8% MoM"

**Expected Outcome:**  
With Tổng Tài financial dashboards, Lan creates compelling investor pitch: MRR $20K, LTV $150, CAC $5, retention 85%, path to $1M ARR by Year 2. Raises Series A successfully.

---

## Cross-Persona Insights

### Common Frustrations (All 5 Personas)

1. **Data Fragmentation:** Scattered across multiple apps/spreadsheets
2. **Manual Work:** Hours spent on reconciliation, analysis, reporting
3. **Lack of Visibility:** Don't know true profitability or cash position
4. **Slow Decisions:** Analysis takes days, then decision becomes outdated
5. **No Guidance:** AI insights would help make better choices

### Common Success Drivers (All 5 Personas)

1. **Real-Time Visibility:** See business metrics instantly
2. **Automated Insights:** AI surfaces problems/opportunities
3. **Unified Data:** Single source of truth across business
4. **Time Savings:** Reduce manual work from hours to minutes
5. **Confidence:** Make decisions with data, not guesswork

## Conclusion

These 5 personas represent the core target market for Tổng Tài. They share common needs (visibility, automation, guidance) but have different priorities based on their business stage and goals. Product roadmap and feature prioritization should ensure all 5 personas succeed, with Phase 1 focusing on their MVP needs:

- **Phương:** Inventory + Opportunity + Journey
- **Tuấn:** Inventory + Forecasting + Reports
- **Hoa:** Finance + Reports + Copilot
- **Anh Minh:** Consumer + Finance + Reports
- **Lan:** Consumer (churn) + Finance (unit economics)
